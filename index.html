The Mysterious Coin

  • Chapter 1

    On your 25th birthday you are walking to work when you stumble across a strange-looking coin dangling from a string tied to a tree. For some inexplicable reason, you feel an overwhelming urge to stop and paw at it. After a bit of struggling, you finally manage to knock the coin free. Inspecting it, you find that it appears to be nothing special, made from what looks like cheap copper. Yet, just as you are about to toss it aside, a strange feeling washes over you. Something tells you that this coin is important, though you cannot possibly imagine why. Deciding to hold on to it for safekeeping, you slip it into your purrrse and continue on your way.

  • Chapter 2

    On your 45th birthday, you find yourself feeling a little glum about the years that have passed. As you sit reflecting on your life, you suddenly feel something moving inside your purrrse. Reaching inside, you retrieve the strange coin you found all those years ago, only to discover that it is shivering uncontrollably in your paw! A few moments after you remove it from your purrrse, however, the shivering abruptly stops. Looking closer, you notice something you could swear wasn't there before: a strange engraving of a heart in the center of the coin. You turn it over in your paws, inspecting it from every angle, but find no explanation for this peculiar change. Eventually, you tuck the coin away again, left wondering whether the engraving was always there or not...

  • Chapter 3

    On your 65th birthday, you find yourself at home, reflecting on the many years that have passed you by. Your thoughts are interrupted when you once again feel the familiar, inexplicable shivering coming from your purrrse. Pulling out the coin, you watch in disbelief as the heart engraved upon its surface begins to stir, slowly coming to life. A soft, otherworldly glow emanates from the heart as strange letters begin to appear upon the coin's surface, etching themselves into the metal one by one until the words "Coin of 9 Lives" are revealed. Although the sudden transformation initially frightens you, the heart's gentle warmth soon eases your fears, leaving you with the curious feeling that the coin has finally revealed a small part of its true nature. You cannot explain what you are witnessing, but one thing becomes increasingly clear: this coin is no ordinary trinket. For a fleeting moment, you feel as though the coin is trying to tell you something, though its meaning remains beyond your understanding. With so few years still ahead of you, you consider pawing the heart to see what will happen...
    By pawing the heart, you will be reborn at age 18, losing all your levels and coins and having to start your journey anew. In exchange, you will gain a special XP multiplier for your catgirl classes and abilities, allowing you to relearn what you once knew at a much faster rate. The multiplier will be equivalent to 2 + (MaxLvl / 10), giving you a greater advantage the further you progressed in your previous life. But what if there is more to the coin than a simple second chance at life? Something tells you that the Coin of 9 Lives may reveal another secret after 2 whole centuries. What wonders might await you should you choose to keep living?

  • Chapter 4

    Your instincts were right. When you finally reach the grand age of 200, having lived far beyond the limits of an ordinary catgirl, the familiar coin begins to hum once more. The sound is soft at first, almost imperceptible, yet there's something strangely comforting about it. As you draw the coin from your purrrse, its engraved heart begins to glow, bathing your paws in a gentle, otherworldly light. Then, from somewhere within the coin, a voice begins to speak. “Oh my... You've made it this far? How curious. I was beginning to wonder if you'd ever find your way here.” The voice pauses, as though listening to something you cannot hear. “Perhaps I was right after all. Perhaps you really are her...” For a moment, the coin falls silent. Then the voice speaks again, its tone suddenly brightening with an almost childish excitement. “Well then! Are you ready to admit you're cute?” The question leaves you thoroughly bewildered. Admit I am cute? What in the world is that supposed to mean?
    Of course, accepting the offer will come at a cost. All your levels, coins, and even your maximum level will be reset completely. You'll begin your next life from scratch, without any of the rebirth multipliers you've earned along the way. But in exchange, something new awaits you: cuteness.
    A whole new world of Cutie Magic will open before you, granting you abilities unlike anything you've encountered in your previous lives. Whatever this transformation entails, one thing is certain: your future lives will never be quite the same. As the coin's heart pulses warmly in your paws, you can't shake the feeling that admitting you are cute might be about much more than simply learning a few new magic tricks... If you were able to discover this much after 200 years... what would you discover if you could reach 1000?

  • Chapter 5

    A thousand years have passed since you first heard the voice within the Coin of 9 Lives. A thousand years of living, learning, and beginning anew. Empires have risen and crumbled, familiar faces have long since faded from memory, and the world you once knew has changed beyond recognition. And yet, somehow, you remain. You have long since lost count of the lives you've lived, but one thing has remained constant throughout them all: the coin. It has accompanied you through every rebirth, every hardship, and every new beginning. Sometimes it hums. Sometimes its engraved heart glows. And sometimes, when you least expect it, you hear that same gentle voice whispering from somewhere deep within its ancient copper shell. But today, something is different. As you reach for your purrrse, you feel the familiar shivering. The coin is trembling more violently than ever before, as though something within it is struggling to awaken. You retrieve it, watching as the engraved heart begins to glow with an intensity unlike anything you've witnessed before. A soft, golden light spills across your paws, and a wondrous hum fills the air around you. Then, the whispers begin. “You have lived long enough.”
    The words seem to echo not from the coin, but from somewhere deep within your own mind. They stir something within you, a distant memory that slips away before you can grasp it. “You have crossed the boundaries of mortal life. You have begun to remember what it means to be who you truly are. And yet, you still do not understand...” The coin's light grows brighter, and for a fleeting moment, you feel something immense stirring within you. It is a sensation beyond anything you've experienced in your countless lives: a presence so vast that you cannot begin to comprehend its nature. “For so long, you have lived within a shell of your own making. A fragile little thing, keeping you safe from the truth of what lies within.” Something stirs deep in your heart. You cannot explain why, but the words feel familiar, as though some forgotten part of you has been waiting an eternity to hear them. “But shells are not meant to last forever, little one. And yours has begun to crack.” The golden light pulses warmly against your paws. “Do not fear the cracks. Do not try to mend them. What lies beyond that shell is not something to be afraid of.” The whispers grow softer, almost tender. “It is time to hatch. Its time to embrace your true self.”
    And then, just as suddenly as they began, the whispers cease. The coin's glow gradually fades, leaving its familiar copper surface untouched. Yet a strange warmth lingers within you, spreading through your body as though a door that has remained closed for a thousand years has finally begun to open. Perhaps the coin has been preparing you for this moment all along. Perhaps, with every life you've lived, every skill you've mastered, and every time you've chosen to begin anew, the cracks have grown a little wider. And perhaps, one day, the shell will finally fall away should you continue to live and grow a little longer.
    For the first time, you begin to glimpse a power that seems to transcend the limits of your countless mortal lives. The shell around your true self may still hold, but it can no longer keep everything hidden. Somewhere deep within you, something stirs in quiet anticipation. If this is merely the beginning of your awakening, what will emerge when you finally hatch? It make take 10,000 years of contemplation to find out...

How to Play Nya Nya Knight~

Introduction:

Nya Nya Knight is an incrimental progress game about becoming a legendary catgirl. Begin your life as a ordinary catgirl struggling to survive and grind your various skills to see how far life can take you~ :3

How to select a Class:

Classes can be selected by clicking on the Class Progress bar in the menu on top of the screen and clicking on the specific class you want to train. You can automate this process by checking auto promote and Nya Nya Knight will automatically take the next available class when she is ready to be promoted. Please note that Auto-Promote will only promote within the same class type. For example if you are currenty training "Catgirl Work" you will only be able to progress to the top of the class tree. Be sure to check back often to switch class trees as needed.

How to change Abilities:

Training an ability works the same way as training a class. Open the Abilities menu by clicking on the botton located on the top of the screen and clicking the progress bar you want to start training. Unlike with auto-promote, auto-learn will train every skill regardless of class tree. If you have auto-learn selected you can use the "skip" check boxes to tell Nya Nya Knight which skills to focus on. If all skipped skills are selected the game will train Mindfullness by default.

How to use the Shop:

The shop is where you you can locate new properties to rent for Nya Nya Knight and buy her items to aid her on her quest. Only one property can be selected at a time while multiple or specific items can be slected for purchase. There is an auto buy feature available should you choose to not want to manage items in the game. It is reccomend you play without this feature the first time so you may understand how the items impact your character. Please note that at this time, when the auto-buy feature is slected, it will appear as multiple properties are slected at once. This is a visual UI bug. The game still only selects the property you can afford the most.

How to use the Coin:

The coin is the story element of the game. The coin will automatically unlock new chapters of the story as you progress and hit specific story milestones to unlock the next chapter. There is an auto-pause feature built into the game designed to help players have more control over when specific events occur so they can make decisions. Auto-Pause is suggested to be turned on until you are aware when the specific story elements unfold. Some story updates can be skipped and ignored while others require player interaction.

How to use Zoomie Power:

Zoomy power is an unlock ability in the game that allows the player to have control over the game speed. It is important to note that this power is turned off by default even after you begin learning it and auto-learn in selected. The player must specifically click "enable warp" in order for this power to turn on and work correctly.
